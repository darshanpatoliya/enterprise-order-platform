# Requirements

## 1. Product Overview

**Enterprise Order & Inventory Platform**

An enterprise-style system for managing customers, products, inventory,
orders, payments, shipments, and notifications.

## 2. Problem

The system provides a centralized platform for managing the complete
order lifecycle while maintaining accurate inventory and enforcing
business rules.

## 3. Users

- Customer
- Administrator
- Inventory Manager
- Warehouse Staff
- Finance Staff

## 4. Initial Functional Requirements

- User registration and authentication
- Customer management
- Product management
- Inventory management
- Order creation and management
- Inventory reservation
- Payment processing
- Shipment management
- Notifications
- Basic reporting
